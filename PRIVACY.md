# Privacy Policy for Tab Purger & Grouper

Last updated: October 12, 2026

## 1. Overview

Tab Purger & Grouper ("the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the extension handles your data.

## 2. Data Collection and Usage

**Tab Purger & Grouper does not collect, track, or transmit any personal data or browsing history.**

- **Tab Information**: The extension accesses tab URLs and titles solely in your local browser memory to detect duplicate tabs and organize them into tab groups. This information is processed strictly on your device and is never logged, stored persistently, or transmitted off your device.
- **Preferences and Settings**: Your extension preferences (such as duplicate matching rules, keep strategy, and grouping options) are stored locally using the Chrome Storage API (`chrome.storage.sync` / `chrome.storage.local`). This data remains strictly within your browser environment and is not accessible to third parties.

## 3. Remote Communications and Analytics

- The extension does not communicate with any external servers or APIs.
- The extension does not use cookies, analytics, telemetry, or advertising scripts.
- The extension does not execute any remote code.

## 4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third parties.

## 5. Security

All operations run locally within the sandboxed extension environment provided by Google Chrome, following Chrome Manifest V3 security standards.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be published in this repository with an updated date.
